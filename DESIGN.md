## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a warm, restorative, and deeply human sanctuary for mental well-being. Departing intentionally from clinical sterility, cold institutional blues, and anxiety-inducing stark whites, the visual language prioritizes psychological safety, breathability, and mindful presence. 

The aesthetic marries **Organic Warm Minimalism** with **Editorial Craft**. It utilizes gentle tactile surfaces, grounding natural hues, and generous spatial cadence to lower the cognitive load and heart rate of visitors. Typography and structural forms balance clinical authority with empathic warmth, welcoming individuals in vulnerable emotional states with dignity, calmness, and quiet optimism.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in biophilic nature—warm botanical greens, soothing linen grounds, and earthen terracotta accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - `canvas`: `#F7F4EE` (Warm linen base; establishes an unhurried, peaceful background atmosphere).
  - `surface-base`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp warm white; used for primary elevated content containers, practitioner cards, and interactive forms).
  - `surface-subtle`: `#EFEAE0` (Deeper neutral tint; used for alternating card sections, secondary containers, and neutral tag backdrops).
- **Primary & Interactive:**
  - `primary`: `#4A6B4A` (Warm restorative leaf green; conveys restoration, grounded healing, and care).
  - `primary-dark`: `#2F4A33` (Deep forest green; provides AAA-accessible contrast for buttons in interactive hover states and active indicators).
- **Accents & Highlights:**
  - `accent-terracotta`: `#C89B7B` (Warm baked clay; reserved for callout highlights, reflective quotes, badges, and nurturing touchpoints).
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - `text-primary`: `#1F2A1F` (Organic charcoal with an undertone of deep pine; softens harsh black while ensuring effortless legibility).
  - `text-muted`: `#6B7A6B` (Soft sage gray-green; ideal for secondary labels, metadata, and supporting narrative copy).
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.08)` (Soft natural delineator; provides structure without imposing rigid, harsh boundaries).

## Typography

The type system blends the literary dignity of **Newsreader** for headings and personal reflection callouts with the clarity of **Manrope** for clinical descriptions, scheduling actions, and structural body text.

- **Editorial Headings (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, introspection, and quiet emotional depth. It avoids sharp angular serifs in favor of gentle proportions and optical nuance. Use italic styles selectively for testimonials, therapist philosophies, and reflective introspections.
- **Supportive Interface & Body (Manrope):** Geometric yet friendly, providing effortless legibility even when readers are experiencing high stress or cognitive fatigue.
- **Hierarchy Guidelines:** Always maintain expansive line height across paragraphs to foster breathing room and cognitive spaciousness. Never set body copy tighter than `1.5`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on generous, rhythmic pacing based on an 8px scale. White space is treated as an active therapeutic element—unhurried layout creates safety and prevents sensory overload.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `2rem` (32px) gutters and max-width container capped at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking lengths. Desktop section vertical margins range between `6rem` (96px) and `7.5rem` (120px).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer canvas margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margins.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Generous breathing gaps between clinical sections (such as therapist bios, treatment modalities, and booking intake).
- **Content Pacing:** Never densely pack text into edge-to-edge layouts; employ gentle asymmetric column offsets (e.g., 5-column text paired with 7-column soft imagery) to create an organic, editorial flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft, organic, atmospheric ambient light rather than harsh structural drops.

- **Canvas Separation:** Natural linen `#F7F4EE` acts as the zero-plane. Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards sit above it, delineated primarily by soft luminance difference and subtle organic shadows.
- **Shadow Character:** Shadows are deeply diffused and tinted with the deep charcoal-green tone (`#1F2A1F`) rather than synthetic neutral black, creating the impression of warm morning light filtering through greenery:
  - **Resting Surface Elevation:** `0 4px 24px rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.06)`
  - **Hovered / Interactive Elevation:** `0 12px 36px rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.08)`
  - **Floating Elements (Modals / Nav Pill):** `0 16px 48px rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.12)`
- **Low-Contrast Structural Borders:** In combination with soft shadows, all cards and layered panels utilize a hairline border of `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.08)` to maintain gentle structural legibility without visual tension.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects organic stones, tumbled river glass, and natural contours:

- **Buttons & Badges:** Strictly pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`, with border radius values set to `9999px`). This eliminates all sharp corners from primary action zones, conveying gentleness and approachable warmth.
- **Cards & Content Surfaces:** 20px (`1.25rem`) corner radius for standard clinic cards, assessment blocks, and interactive containers.
- **Hero Wrappers & Imagery Frames:** 24px–28px (`1.5rem`–`1.75rem`) corner radius for large environmental photography, therapist portraits, and warm sanctuary vignettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with `#4A6B4A`, typography in crisp white `#FFFFFF` (`label-md`). Hover state gently shifts to `#2F4A33` with smooth ease transitions (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over 200ms).
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped with a transparent core or `#F7F4EE` tint, surrounded by `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.15)`, text in `#1F2A1F`. On hover, fills subtly with `#EFEAE0`.
- **Terracotta Accent CTA:** Pill-shaped button filled with `#C89B7B` and white text, reserved exclusively for emotional reassurance, direct booking triggers, or emergency crisis support links.

### Chips & Badges
- Continuous pill shape with internal padding `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal.
- **Botanical Variant:** Background `rgba(74, 107, 74, 0.12)`, text `#2F4A33`. Used for therapeutic specialties (e.g., "Cognitive Behavioral Therapy", "Mindfulness").
- **Warm Terracotta Variant:** Background `rgba(200, 155, 123, 0.16)`, text `#8A5C3C`. Used for availability status (e.g., "Accepting New Clients").

### Cards & Sanctuary Surfaces
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `20px`, enclosed in `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.08)`, elevated by soft ambient drop shadow.
- Interior card padding is generous: `space-lg` (32px) on mobile scaling to `space-xl` (48px) on desktop to guarantee breathing space around bios and service descriptions.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Generously proportioned with a minimum height of 52px to facilitate calm, unhurried interactions.
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.12)`, border-radius `12px`. Focus state shifts the border to `#4A6B4A` with a soft green ambient halo (`0 0 0 4px rgba(74, 107, 74, 0.12)`).
- Labels are persistent, high-legibility `Manrope` set in `#1F2A1F` with helper text in `#6B7A6B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px dimensional controls. Radios have full circle radius; checkboxes have a soft 6px radius.
- Unchecked border `1.5px solid rgba(31, 42, 31, 0.25)`. Checked state transitions seamlessly into `#4A6B4A` background with crisp white checkmark/dot indicators.

### Testimonial & Reflection Callouts
- Wide editorial cards rendered in `#EFEAE0` surface tint with a left-aligned organic border or quotation motif in `#C89B7B`.
- Features `Newsreader` italic typography (`quote-editorial`) followed by a grounded `Manrope` attribution block in `#6B7A6B`.
